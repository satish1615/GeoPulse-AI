How the scenario index works

Every output uses an explicit, reproducible equation. Baseline city scores are synthetic teaching values. The city point coordinates refer to real locations, while the heat, flood and combined scores are assumptions for experimentation.

Heat = clamp(baseHeat + 12 × Δ°C − 0.60 × canopy%)Flood = clamp(baseFlood + 0.55 × rainfall% − 0.18 × canopy%)Combined = 0.55 × Heat + 0.45 × Flood

All scores are capped at 0–100. Reference scenario: 0°C warming, 0% rainfall change and 10% canopy. Geographic outlines: simplified Natural Earth map for context. These results should not be used for operational climate, engineering or public-safety decisions.
